**Full description:**
Learn with Lili from the Lili & Friends channel in four calm games for children aged 4 to 7:

🌟 Lili's Path — tap arrows and press Go, and Lili walks to the star. Sequencing, planning and fixing mistakes: the first ideas of coding.
🐸 Lili Hops — Lili hops along numbered stones; your child guesses where she lands. Counting, adding and taking away up to 20.
🔷 Pattern Machine — what comes next? Spot patterns, build your own, and Lili claps along.
🍎 Balance Scale — add fruit until both sides weigh the same. Comparing, equality and early algebra.

Why parents like it:
• A profile for each child — several children on one device, each with their own progress.
• A journey map for every game: replay finished levels any time; the next ones open when they get there.
• Calm by design: short rounds with a clear end, no timers, no penalties, hints after two tries.
• Adapts to your child: moves up when they're ready, practises more when they need it.
• Completely private: no ads, no internet, no data collected, no permissions.
• Runs on old and new phones and tablets (Android 4.4 and up).
• In Arabic and English.
