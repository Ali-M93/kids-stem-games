<p>A friendly machine shows a row of shapes, such as circle, triangle, circle, triangle, ?, and the child taps the one that comes next. Spotting patterns is the root of algebra.</p>
<ul>
<li>Every shape sings its own note, so each pattern is also a little tune that the machine plays back when solved</li>
<li>Stages from AB to AAB, ABB, ABC, colour-only patterns, gaps in the middle, AABB and two missing shapes</li>
<li>A wrong pick wobbles and greys out; after two, the repeats are underlined</li>
<li>Make mode: build your own pattern, and the machine finds your rule and carries on with it</li>
<li>A sticker every five puzzles; moves up a stage when the child is ready</li>
</ul>
<p><b>No permissions at all: no network, no ads, no tracking.</b></p>
